Everything the drawing gives you

Draw the job once. QuickPlumb pulls the sizing, the coordinates, the fittings and the numbers straight off your drawing.

Import the floor plan

Drop in the architect's plan — PDF or almost any image format — and use it as the backdrop for walls, fixtures and pipe.

Stub-ups from the forms

Highlight a fixture and dimension lines stretch from its drain connection to every reference line on the drawing. No more cutting concrete.

Automatic fitting recognition

Every fitting is recognised as you attach pipe. Tees, ells, couplings — counted as you draw, so you never show up short.

Auto water pipe sizing

Enter the available pressure, hit Go, and watch QuickPlumb size the water distribution using accepted engineering practice based on the IPC and UPC.

3D views and isometrics

Switch between plan, front, side and iso. Orbit the model, print the isometric for the field, and hand inspectors the drawing they ask for.

Bill of materials

A materials list built from your drawing and your customer selections — printable, or uploaded to a participating supply house for a live quote.

Pipe cut list

Pre-cut on the chop saw and match each numbered pipe to the same number on the printed drawing. Less waste, faster assembly.

Estimates with labor

Quantities and prices from the drawing, plus labor from your own install times and hourly rate. Edit lines right on the estimate before it prints.

Who builds it

Written by people who
ran the pipe first.

Epiphany Software Corporation was started by people with plumbing design backgrounds, working alongside plumbing contractors. We know first-hand what goes wrong in the field and what goes wrong in the office.

Most software vendors overlook the problems that actually cost a plumbing contractor money — getting accurate coordinates for stub-ups in slab construction, counting fittings so nobody makes a supply-house run, keeping the field crew and the drawing in agreement. Those are the problems QuickPlumb was built to solve.

There is no substitute for proper planning. With QuickPlumb you can relax knowing the job will come together just as planned.
